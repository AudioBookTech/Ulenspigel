**Ulenspigel** is an open-source Android audiobook application demonstrating cutting-edge, AI-powered **Google Lyra** speech compression technology. 

Built in Android Studio, this project delivers a standalone audiobook player engineered for maximum storage efficiency. By leveraging the innovative Lyra neural audio codec, the app occupies minimal storage space on mobile devices while maintaining remarkable speech clarity and natural voice quality at ultra-low bitrates.

---

## 🌟 The "Powered by Google Lyra" Ecosystem

This project is an integral part of the **Powered by Google Lyra** suite, designed to demonstrate the practical application of neural audio compression:

* 🎛️ **[LyraCodec](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9mz8kt923n6l?hl=en-US&gl=US)**  
  A modern, lightweight utility designed specifically for high-efficiency audio encoding and decoding. Built on the state-of-the-art Google Lyra architecture, this app allows you to seamlessly convert standard audio formats into ultra-low-bitrate Lyra files and back, maintaining impressive speech quality even at incredibly small file sizes.

* 📖 **[Ulenspigel](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.KonstantinShramko.Ulenspigel)** *(This project)*  
  A standalone, showcase Android audiobook application demonstrating client-side playback of Lyra-encoded audiobooks with minimal storage footprint.

* 📚 **[Lyra Books](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.KonstantinShramko.LyraBooks)**  
  A specialized Audiobook Hub and launcher designed to unify standalone audiobook applications into a single, elegant interface. The app's core mission is to overcome the significant limitation of short system app labels—which are often truncated to just 8–10 characters—by providing users with full, descriptive audiobook titles and detailed author metadata. By automatically identifying compatible book-apps, **Lyra Books** transforms a fragmented collection of cryptic icons into a professional, curated digital library where every book is easily identifiable and properly organized.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **AI-Powered Compression:** Employs the Google Lyra codec for ultra-low bitrate audio transmission and storage.
* **Minimal Footprint:** Saves valuable device storage without sacrificing speech intelligibility.
* **Standalone Playback:** Complete player architecture built natively for Android.
* **Seamless Integration:** Compatible with the **Lyra Books** library ecosystem.
